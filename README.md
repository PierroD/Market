# Lindra Market v3

Call of Duty Market (v3) is the next-generation FPS Unlocker and game tweaking tool for classic and modern Call of Duty titles, completely redesigned with **.NET 10**, **WPF**, and a modern **Fluent Design** interface.

---

## Supported Games
- **Call of Duty 4: Modern Warfare** (`iw3mp.exe`) - x86
- **Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 2** (`iw4mp.exe` / `iw4sp.exe`) : 2.0.13 - x64 
- **Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 3** (`iw5mp.exe`) : 2.0.12 - x64
- **Call of Duty: Black Ops** (`BlackOpsMP.exe`) - x86
- **Call of Duty: Black Ops II** (`t6mp.exe` / `t6sp.exe` / `t6zm.exe`) : (447) 43.1736.11 / (447) 43.0.-1.0 / 43.1701.1 - x86
- **Call of Duty: Black Ops III** (Custom Maps Language Selector)

---

## Installation & Usage

1. **Download the latest release** from [GitHub Releases](https://github.com/PierroD/Market/releases/latest).
2. Unzip the standalone archive anywhere on your computer.
3. Launch `Lindra Market v3.exe`.
4. Launch your game, adjust your FOV, FPS cap, visuals, or console settings, and enjoy!

> [!NOTE]
> The release archive is **standalone (Self-Contained)**: no extra .NET runtime installation is required.

---

## Features
- **FPS & FOV Unlocker**: Real-time adjustment of Field of View, FOV scale, minimum FOV, and maximum FPS limit.
- **Visuals Tweak**: Toggle lightmaps, specular maps, movie mode, fog, glow, bullet marks, and weapon camos.
- **External Console**: In-game command execution and live Dvar catalog browser.
- **BO3 Language Selector**: Fix crashes when loading custom maps missing your localized game language.
- **Themes & Localization**: Fluent Light/Dark/System theme support with French and English localizations.
